Questions

Before you ask.

What do I need to start?

Your people and what they cost, your projects with their fees and dates, and who works on what. Most studios model themselves in an afternoon.

Is it AI?

The numbers are arithmetic on data you already have. You can ask questions in plain words, and the answer points to the people and projects it came from.

How is it different from a project management tool?

Those record what happened: timesheets, invoices, utilization reports. Prizma computes what happens next, before you commit to it.

What is a pilot?

We set up your studio's model with you, on your own numbers, and you try it on the next project you are offered.
